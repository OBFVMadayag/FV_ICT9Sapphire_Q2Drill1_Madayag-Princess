A Dodgeball Guide for Beginners and First Timers



Welcome to the beginner's guide in dodgeball! You can learn the basics of playing dodgeball, the rules and violations in playing dodgeball, the equipment used in dodgeball, and the importance of playing dodgeball.
How To Play
  1. Gather 3 or more people in one team.
  2. Arrange the dodgeballs at the center of the court.
  3. Set a 10 minute timer.
  4. Rush the balls at the center of the court as soon as the game begins.
  5. Win by tagging all of the opposing players out.
Rules In Dodgeball
  1. A standard court in dodgeball is a 40 yard by 20 yard rectangle with a line dividing it into two squares.
  2. Identify the waiting area on each side of the court.
  3. For kindergarteners, the court is 20 yards by 10 yards.
  4. To make sure all players are safe, create a buffer zone in which players cannot get hit about four feet of the line.
Violations In Dodgeball
  1. Boundary Violation
  2. Stalling/Ball Control
  3. Illegal Kicking
  4. Equipment Abuse
Equipment In Dodgeball
  1. Dodgeballs
  2. Court Markers
  3. Referee Shirts
  4. Whistles
  5. Nets
  6. Radar Guns
Importance Of Learning Dodgeball
  1. Enhances reflexes
  2. Boosts physical fitness
  3. Develops teamwork skills
  4. Promotes socialization
  5. Builds confidence
